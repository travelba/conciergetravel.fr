separately and are **out of scope** for the target gate.

## Pillar / combinator reconciliation

- **Combinator seeds** derive `targetLength` from scope (city/cluster/country/theme). The
  `--strict` gate in `run-rankings-v2-bulk.ts` now refuses to push a freshly-generated seed
  whose `entries.length` is below that target.
- **Pillar / curated rankings** (chain rankings, Travel+Leisure World’s Best, hand-built
  allow-lists) are pushed by dedicated runners with an explicit entry list; their "target" is
  the allow-list size. They have no combinator seed, so they appear in the _unmatched_ bucket
  above and are intentionally exempt from the combinator target gate — the runner that
  owns the allow-list is the source of truth for their completeness.
- The shared `completeness.ts` helper (`isComplete` / `completenessStatus` /
  `summarizeCompleteness`) is the single contract used by the bulk gate, `verify-rankings.ts`
  and this report, removing the historic per-script drift.
